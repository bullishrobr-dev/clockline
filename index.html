Each person needs their own PIN to use the clock. PINs are stored scrambled, so write a new one down when you set it. If someone forgets theirs, set a new one. Removing a person keeps their past hours in reports.

How the clock works

Staff can only clock in on a shop device set up under Locations. On any other phone or computer the clock asks for a location password and shows no keypad. They type their PIN and pick check in, break, back or check out, and never see hours, theirs or anyone else's. Every tap is stored with the server's time and the shop it came from. Admin works from anywhere and signs out after 15 minutes without use.
